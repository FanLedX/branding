# Voice and messaging

Part of the Fan Led X brand set. See [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

The source is the strategy deck (slides 1, 3, 4, 5 and 16). Quoted lines are **verbatim**:
don't paraphrase them in headline positions.

## 1. Positioning

Fan Led is the sports and events arm of CLX Professionals. CLX's expertise comes from
retail: operational excellence in high-volume environments, human-centred design (zoning,
layout, staff training), technology integration from hardware to data, and change
management. Fan Led applies it to sport, where, in the roadmap's words, organisations are
"10 years behind retail in understanding customers".

> "An experience-led operational strategy and delivery partner, for sports and events.
> It bridges operational performance and human experience, helping organisations move from
> strategic ambition to real-world delivery. We design systems around how people move,
> feel and behave, connecting strategy, experience, infrastructure, technology and
> delivery into one coherent operating model."

The quote above is written in the third person. When it goes into first-person copy,
"It bridges…" becomes "We bridge…".

## 2. Messaging hierarchy

Each level has a job. Use the right one for the slot.

| Level | Line | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Market position | "An experience-led operational strategy and delivery partner for sports and events." | Meta descriptions, boilerplate, intros, kickers |
| Core proposition (short) | "We design and deliver smarter, safer and more resilient operations, built around how people move, feel and behave." | Sub-heads, page intros, repeated use |
| Core proposition (long) | "Fan Led helps sports and event organisations design and deliver smarter, safer and more commercially resilient operations, built around how people actually move, feel and behave. From arrival to exit, we reduce friction, improve flow and create live environments that work in the real world." | A homepage or cover intro, used once |
| Commercial offer | "End-to-End Sports & Event Solutions. One Accountable Delivery Partner." | Buyer-facing headlines and CTAs |
| Tagline | "Operations built around people. Experiences that turn guests into fans." | Hero headline, closing statement, footer, sign-off |
| Belief | "Operational systems are human systems." | A standalone statement moment |
| Methodology | "Operational Behavioural Design." | The method's name. Always in title case |

**The pillar mantra:** "Strategy sets the direction. Experience shapes the journey. Delivery
brings it to life." Each clause can introduce its own pillar.

The pillar names are **not yet consistent** across materials:

| Source | Pillar 1 | Pillar 2 | Pillar 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deck mantra | Strategy | Experience | Delivery |
| Website, Home | Strategy, Commercial & Operating Models | Infrastructure | Experience |
| Website, Services | Strategy | Technology | Delivery |

Strategy is always first. Until the client settles the names, keep to one set within a
piece of work. Use the mantra set for spoken or brand-level copy and the Services set when
listing services.

**Built around:** Movement · Trust · Safety · Service · Resilience · Commercial performance ·
Return intent. These work as a recurring list (chips, a marquee, a slide) rather than
buried in prose.

## 3. Naming

| Context | Form |
|---|---|
| Running copy | **Fan Led**, e.g. "Fan Led works across sports venues…" |
| Brand name, titles, meta, first mention in a document | **Fan Led X** |
| Abbreviation | **FLX**. For internal use, filenames and tight spaces. Not in body copy |
| Legal entity line | "© 2026 Fan Led Experience. All rights reserved." |
| Parent | "Fan Led is the sports & events arm of CLX Professionals." |
| Method | Operational Behavioural Design |

Write "Fan Led" as two words, each capitalised. Never "FanLed", "Fan-Led" or "Fanled". The X is
part of the mark and the full name, not a separate word in copy.

## 4. Tone of voice

> "Fan Led should sound as clear and confident as the work itself. It is premium,
> practical and founder-led."

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Clear before clever** | Simple sentences. Strong lines. |
| **Human before technical** | Systems matter when people trust them. |
| **Operational before aesthetic** | Explain movement, friction and confidence. |
| **Founder-led** | Tony speaks as a practitioner. |
| **No fluff** | Avoid vague consultancy language. |
| **Practical before abstract** | Every message connects back to a real operational outcome. |

## 5. Writing rules

- **Short declarative sentences**, one idea each. The house rhythm is the tricolon of
  short sentences: *"One fan path. One connected operation. One experience."*
- **People first, system second.** Say what happens to fans, staff and partners, then how.
- **Name the concrete thing:** queues, gates, turnstiles, wayfinding, concourses, dwell
  time, arrival, entry, exit, handover, staffing pressure, F&B.
- **The contrast move:** how organisations are built versus how people experience them.
  *"Fans don't experience your organisation in departments. They experience it as one
  journey."*
- **The signature triplet "move, feel and behave":** once per page or document section at
  most, and never twice on one screen or slide.
- **End on an outcome**, not a feature.
- **Headlines** are sentence case and usually end with a full stop. A headline can run to
  two or three short sentences: *"Three connected pillars. One operating system. Built
  around people."*
- **British English:** organisation, optimise, centre, programme.
- **No em dashes.** Use full stops, commas or " - ".
- **Banned words:** synergy, leverage, holistic, best-in-class, world-class,
  solutions-driven, "unlock" as jargon.

### Before and after (illustrative, not client copy)

| Avoid | Write |
|---|---|
| "We leverage holistic, best-in-class solutions to unlock fan engagement." | "We shorten the queue at the gate so fans arrive in the ground, not outside it." |
| "A world-class end-to-end experience platform." | "One accountable partner from strategy to the day itself." |

## 6. Proof and numbers

Cite numbers with their source in brackets, as the roadmap does.

- 24% of fans are frustrated by toilet wait times (Gemba, 2024)
- 19% are frustrated by F&B queues (Gemba, 2024)
- 56% would use an app to order food and drink (Gemba, 2024)
- 38% would stay longer after an event if there were more options (Gemba, 2024)
- Only 50% believe live events offer an unmatched experience (Deloitte, 2026)
- The Middle East sports market is growing at 8.7% (2024 to 2026, PwC). The Saudi sports
  sector is projected to reach $22.4bn by 2030 (PwC).

Case studies in use: **Rod Laver Arena** (Melbourne) and **Camborne RFC** (Cornwall).

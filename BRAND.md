## The brand in one page

**Who:** Fan Led is the sports and events arm of **CLX Professionals**. CLX learned to move
people through high-volume retail spaces without friction. Fan Led brings that to
venues, events, clubs, fan zones and community sport. The offices are in London, Dubai and
Melbourne.

**Tagline:** *Operations built around people. Experiences that turn guests into fans.*

**Proposition:** *We design and deliver smarter, safer and more resilient operations,
built around how people move, feel and behave.*

**Pillars:** Strategy sets the direction. Experience shapes the journey. Delivery brings it
to life. (The pillar names vary between materials. See VOICE.md.)

**Voice:** premium, practical and founder-led. Clear before clever. Short declarative
sentences. Concrete operational nouns. No consultancy fluff. No em dashes.

**Colour:** black, navy, electric blue `#1930FF` and signal lime `#C7FF13`.
Dark grounds for impact, cool light grey grounds for content. Lime is a signal, never a
large fill.

**Type:** Inter. Headlines in Black (900), in sentence case with a full stop. Uppercase only
for eyebrows, buttons, nav and statement bands.

**Mark:** an X made of slanted strands running lime to blue, which suggests crowds and flow.

**Imagery:** real people moving through real venues. Editorial, human and operational.
Never empty stadiums.

# Imagery and graphic system

Part of the Fan Led X brand set. See [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).

The deck's brief: *"The identity translates movement, pressure and people into a
recognisable brand. Bold and modern, but grounded in real environments. The result should
be premium, modern and distinctly fan led."*

---

## Photography

> "Editorial, human and operational." "Real people and real movement, not empty venues."

**Use:**
- Crowds moving through concourses, tunnels and gates
- Wayfinding signage, turnstiles, entry lanes, retail and F&B outlets
- Staff at work, and teams working over plans and drawings
- Real venues with something happening in them

**Avoid:**
- Empty stadium beauty shots
- Abstract tech renders, glowing data or holograms
- Stock consultants posing to camera, handshakes
- Generic sports action with no operational story

**Treatment:**
- **On dark grounds:** low-key and moody, often shot from behind the crowd, in deep shadow
  with venue lighting. Add a black scrim so the text stays legible.
- **On light grounds:** natural colour, cropped into cards with rounded corners (about 10px).
- Don't use colour filters, duotones or overlays in brand colours.

## Graphic system

The deck's brief: *"Direction, flow, movement, pressure."*

| Element | Description | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The X** | Slanted gradient strands. See LOGO.md | Hero and cover graphic, footer, sign-off |
| **Vertical bars** | Rows of thin vertical bars of varying height, lime at the top fading to blue, like a crowd-density or pressure reading | Section bands and dividers, slide backgrounds |
| **Rule lines** | Short lime underlines beneath headlines and labels, and lime vertical bars beside pull headings | Under headlines, beside headings |
| **Arrows** | → in every action. Circular outline buttons containing an arrow | CTAs, "next" cues, cards |
| **Dotted map** | A halftone dot world map with blue glows and lime-highlighted markets (UK, UAE, Australia) | Presence and offices |
| **Icons** | Electric-blue outline line icons, either standalone or in a rounded square | Feature lists, pillar cards |

**Principles**
- Motion is implied by **direction**: diagonals, left-to-right, things in sequence.
- Keep graphics flat. Gradients belong only to the X and the bars.
- Corners: rounded for images and cards (10–27px), fully round for buttons. Panels and
  bands are square.
